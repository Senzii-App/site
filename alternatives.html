Why Teams Switch to Senzii

Most scheduling software gives you a digital calendar and leaves the hard work to you. Senzii is different — it uses AI to automatically match the best staff member to each shift based on skills, proximity to the work site, certifications, and performance history. No more spending hours assigning shifts manually.

Senzii is free and open-source under the Apache License 2.0. No tiers, no per-seat fees, no per-location fees, no feature gates. Self-host it or use the hosted version — same features either way. You can even talk to your schedule through ChatGPT or Claude via MCP integration.

When I Work Alternative

When I Work is a solid scheduling tool with auto-scheduling, a built-in time clock, and broad integrations. But it charges $2.50-$8/user/month and lacks AI shift matching.

  • AI matching by skills & proximity (WIW has auto-scheduling, not AI matching)
  • Free and open-source (WIW charges per user)
  • Coverage gap detection (WIW doesn't offer)
  • MCP integration — manage schedules via ChatGPT/Claude

Which Alternative Is Right for You?

Switch from When I Work if:

  • You want AI to match the best person to each shift based on skills and proximity
  • You want free and open-source instead of paying $2.50-$8/user/month
  • You need coverage gap detection or shift bidding
  • You want to manage schedules by talking to ChatGPT or Claude

Stay with When I Work if you need a built-in time clock, team messaging, or specific integrations.

Switch from Deputy if:

  • You want AI-powered shift matching instead of manual auto-scheduling
  • You want free and open-source instead of paying $4.50-$9/user/month
  • You need coverage gap detection or shift bidding
  • You want to manage schedules by talking to AI assistants

Stay with Deputy if you need a biometric time clock, task management, compliance monitoring, or broad payroll integrations.

Switch from Sling if:

  • You want AI-powered shift matching (Sling has none)
  • You want free scheduling with no 30-user cap
  • You need coverage gap detection, shift bidding, or certification tracking
  • You want to manage schedules by talking to AI assistants

Stay with Sling if you need built-in time tracking, team messaging, or native mobile apps.

Switch from Homebase if:

  • You want AI-powered shift matching without paying for Plus
  • You want free scheduling with no per-location fees
  • You need coverage gap detection, shift bidding, or MCP integration
  • You prefer open-source software you can self-host

Stay with Homebase if you need hiring/onboarding, integrated payroll, a time clock, or POS integrations.
