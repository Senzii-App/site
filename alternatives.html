Which Alternative Is Right for You?

Switch from When I Work if:

  • You're spending hours manually building schedules and want AI to do the heavy lifting
  • You're paying extra for compliance features that should be included
  • You need instant shift coverage when someone calls out (no group texts)
  • You want predictable per-seat pricing instead of opaque tier upgrades

Switch from Deputy if:

  • You're paying premium prices but only need scheduling (not time-clock with facial recognition)
  • You want AI scheduling without upgrading to a higher tier
  • Your costs are scaling unpredictably with team growth
  • You want a simpler interface without the clutter of features you don't use

Switch from Sling if:

  • Your team has grown past 10-15 people and manual scheduling is eating your week
  • You need compliance monitoring to avoid labor law violations
  • You want traffic-based analytics to optimize staffing levels
  • You need API access for custom integrations

Switch from Homebase if:

  • You want AI-powered scheduling without paying for bundled HR/hiring/payroll features
  • You need better analytics and reporting than Homebase's basic tier
  • You want instant shift coverage for call-outs
  • You prefer per-seat pricing over per-location pricing
