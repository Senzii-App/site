How Senzii solves caregiver scheduling

Smart caregiver-patient matching

Senzii automatically matches caregivers to patients based on:

  • Skills & certifications — CNA, HHA, LPN, RN matched to patient care needs
  • Proximity — caregivers assigned to nearby patients to minimize travel time
  • Availability — real-time availability tracking prevents double-booking
  • Patient preferences — language, gender, and continuity of care preferences
  • Certification status — expired certifications flagged before assignment

Instant call-out coverage

When a caregiver calls out, Senzii automatically:

  • Identifies the uncovered visit and patient requirements
  • Searches for qualified, available caregivers within proximity
  • Sends instant notifications to eligible replacements
  • Confirms the replacement and updates the schedule
  • Notifies the patient of the caregiver change

Average time to fill a call-out: under 4 minutes vs. 45+ minutes manually.

EVV-ready time tracking

Senzii supports Electronic Visit Verification compliance with:

  • Caregiver check-in/check-out time logging
  • Location verification at patient site
  • Visit duration calculation for billing
  • Audit trail for Medicaid reporting
  • Export to billing and payroll systems

Route optimization

For multi-visit caregivers, Senzii optimizes daily routes to minimize drive time:

  • Optimal patient visit ordering based on location
  • Drive time calculation between visits
  • Geographic clustering for efficient territory management
  • Reduced caregiver fatigue and mileage reimbursement costs

Frequently asked questions

What is caregiver scheduling software?

Caregiver scheduling software is a digital tool that helps home care agencies manage caregiver shifts, patient assignments, and visit schedules. It automates the matching of caregivers to patients based on skills, certifications, proximity, and availability, reducing manual scheduling work by up to 70%.

How much does caregiver scheduling software cost?

Senzii costs $5 per seat per month with no long-term contract. This is significantly less than competitors like ClearCare ($200+/month) or Homecare Homebase ($300+/month). A 14-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

Can caregiver scheduling software reduce no-shows?

Yes. Automated scheduling software reduces no-shows by 30-40% through real-time shift notifications, automatic coverage gap detection, and predictive matching. When a caregiver calls out, the system instantly identifies qualified replacements based on proximity and availability.

Does Senzii support EVV (Electronic Visit Verification)?

Senzii tracks caregiver check-in and check-out times, which supports EVV compliance requirements. The system logs visit arrival and departure times, providing audit trails for Medicaid billing and compliance reporting.

How does caregiver scheduling software handle last-minute call-outs?

When a caregiver calls out, Senzii automatically searches for qualified replacements based on patient compatibility (skills, language, gender preferences), geographic proximity, and availability. The system sends instant notifications to eligible caregivers and fills the shift in minutes instead of hours.

Can caregiver scheduling software integrate with payroll and billing?

Senzii exports time and attendance data for payroll processing and supports integration with QuickBooks, ADP, and other payroll systems via API. Visit hours are automatically calculated from check-in/check-out data for accurate billing.
