Features

Everything a church coordinator needs

📅

Volunteer Rotation Scheduling

Create recurring rotations for greeters, ushers, worship team, nursery, and tech team. Set cycle patterns (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly) and Senzii fills the calendar automatically based on volunteer availability.

⛪

Multi-Service Scheduling

Manage separate volunteer teams for each service — 8 AM traditional, 10 AM contemporary, 6 PM evening. The system prevents double-booking and tracks who serves where each week.

🎵

Worship Team Coordination

Schedule band members, vocalists, and AV techs with role-based assignments. Track who plays which instrument, ensure balanced team rotations, and prevent the same person from being scheduled every week.

👶

Nursery & Childcare Rotation

Rotate nursery workers with background-check tracking and certification expiry alerts. Ensure minimum staffing ratios per service and automatically flag weeks where coverage falls short.

📱

Automated Reminders

SMS and email reminders go out before each service. Volunteers confirm or request a swap directly from the message — no app download required. Cut no-shows by up to 80%.

🔄

Swap & Availability Portal

Volunteers set their availability, request swaps with qualified replacements, and mark preferences for specific roles or service times. Coordinators approve with one click. No more group text chains.

🏛️

Event & Facility Scheduling

Schedule Bible studies, youth group, retreats, VBS, and community events. Reserve rooms, assign coordinators, track setup/teardown crews, and prevent facility conflicts.

✅

Conflict Detection

Senzii automatically flags double-booked volunteers, insufficient coverage for any role, gaps in the rotation, and volunteers who haven't served in a configurable number of weeks.

💬

Built-in Communication

Send messages to specific ministry teams or the entire volunteer corps. Broadcast schedule changes, event announcements, or urgent coverage needs. Two-way SMS included.
