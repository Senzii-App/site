Why Senzii for cleaning

Everything a cleaning business needs to schedule efficiently

From crew dispatch to route optimization, Senzii handles the scheduling complexity so your operations team can focus on growth.

🧹

Crew Assignment Scheduling

Assign the right crew to each job based on skills, certifications, and proximity. Commercial, residential, post-construction, biohazard — Senzii matches crews to job requirements automatically.

🗺️

Route Optimization

Minimize drive time by clustering nearby jobs. Senzii suggests the most efficient route order and flags backtracking. Set geographic zones to keep crews in one area and reduce fuel costs.

🔄

Recurring Client Scheduling

Set daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly recurring schedules in seconds. Senzii auto-generates future appointments and adjusts when clients pause, skip, or change frequency.

📍

Proximity-Based Dispatch

When an emergency job comes in, Senzii shows which crews are nearby and available. Dispatch the closest qualified crew and reshuffle recurring appointments with one click.

⏱️

GPS Time Tracking

Crews clock in and out at each job site with GPS verification. Senzii tracks actual time on site vs. estimated time, helping you improve quoting accuracy and payroll precision.

📦

Supply & Equipment Tracking

Assign equipment (vacuums, floor scrubbers, pressure washers) to crews. Track supply levels and schedule restocking. Get alerts when equipment needs maintenance or calibration.

✅

Quality Inspection Scheduling

Schedule post-cleaning quality inspections automatically. Assign inspectors to completed jobs, track pass/fail rates, and flag recurring issues at specific client sites.

🏢

Multi-Site Coordination

Manage crews across multiple buildings, neighborhoods, or cities. Senzii tracks which crew is where and prevents gaps in coverage at critical client sites.

📱

Client Notifications

Automatically notify clients when crews are en route, on site, and finished. Send before/after photos and inspection reports directly from the field. Reduce "are you coming?" calls.
