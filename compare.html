Head-to-Head Comparisons

Senzii vs When I Work

When I Work is a well-established player with a clean interface and broad feature set. It's a solid choice if you want manual scheduling with good team communication.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching (When I Work has no AI scheduling)
  • Traffic-based staffing that matches staff to demand
  • Instant shift coverage for call-outs (no group texts needed)
  • Labor law compliance included at no extra cost (When I Work charges extra)
  • Simple per-seat pricing — $5/seat means a 25-person team pays $125/mo vs $60+ for When I Work

Where When I Work wins:

  • More integrations available out of the box
  • Larger user community and more third-party reviews
  • Marketplace for add-on features

Verdict: If you want AI to do the heavy lifting and save on compliance costs, Senzii is the better choice. If you need specific integrations or prefer a more manual approach, When I Work is worth considering.

Senzii vs Deputy

Deputy is a comprehensive platform with strong time-and-attendance features and good compliance tools. It's popular with larger teams.

Where Senzii wins:

  • Dramatically lower cost — $500/mo for 100 employees vs $450+/mo for Deputy, with AI scheduling included
  • AI-powered shift matching included at $5/seat
  • Traffic-based staffing analytics
  • Instant shift coverage for call-outs
  • Simpler interface with less clutter

Where Deputy wins:

  • Built-in time clock with facial recognition
  • More mature integrations ecosystem
  • Stronger time-and-attendance compliance features
  • Larger user base and more reviews

Verdict: Deputy is stronger if you need advanced time-clock features. For AI scheduling at a fraction of the cost, Senzii is the clear winner. Many teams use Senzii for scheduling and a separate time clock, saving hundreds per month.

Senzii vs Sling

Sling by Toast is a budget-friendly option with a free tier. It's popular in food service and retail.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching (Sling has none)
  • Traffic-based staffing analytics
  • Labor law compliance monitoring
  • Overtime prevention alerts
  • Instant shift coverage for call-outs
  • API access (included with $5/seat plan)

Where Sling wins:

  • Free tier for very small teams
  • Lower entry price point
  • Built-in news feed for team communication

Verdict: Sling's free tier is nice for getting started, but it lacks AI scheduling, compliance monitoring, and traffic analytics. Once your team grows past 10-15 people, Senzii's automation saves more than the price difference.

Senzii vs Homebase

Homebase focuses on hourly teams with hiring, scheduling, and payroll features bundled together.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching and traffic-based staffing
  • Lower cost for scheduling-focused teams ($5/seat vs $60+)
  • Better analytics and reporting at lower tiers
  • Instant shift coverage for call-outs
  • API access (included with $5/seat plan)

Where Homebase wins:

  • Built-in hiring and onboarding tools
  • Integrated payroll processing
  • Team communication features
  • Larger template library

Verdict: If you want an all-in-one HR platform, Homebase covers more ground. If you specifically want AI-powered scheduling that saves hours every week, Senzii does that better and cheaper.
