Senzii vs When I Work

When I Work is a well-established player with a clean interface, broad integrations, and auto-scheduling. It's a solid choice for manual scheduling with team communication.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching by skills, proximity, and certifications (When I Work has auto-scheduling but not AI matching)
  • Coverage gap detection (When I Work doesn't offer this)
  • Shift bidding functionality
  • MCP integration — manage schedules via ChatGPT or Claude
  • Free and open-source — no per-user fees, no tiers

Where When I Work wins:

  • Built-in time clock with GPS and geofencing (Senzii doesn't have a time clock yet)
  • More integrations available out of the box (Rippling, POS systems)
  • Native iOS and Android apps (Senzii is mobile web)
  • Larger user community and more third-party reviews

Verdict: If you want AI to match the best person to each shift based on skills and proximity, Senzii is the better choice — and it's free. If you need a time clock, specific integrations, or a mature platform with native apps, When I Work is worth considering.

Senzii vs Deputy

Deputy is a comprehensive platform with strong time-and-attendance features, biometric time clock, and broad payroll integrations. It's popular with larger teams.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching by skills, proximity, and certifications (Deputy has auto-scheduling but not AI matching)
  • Free and open-source vs Deputy's $6.50-$9/user/month
  • Coverage gap detection
  • Shift bidding functionality
  • MCP integration — manage schedules via AI assistants
  • Simpler interface with less clutter

Where Deputy wins:

  • Built-in time clock with GPS, biometrics (facial recognition), and geofencing
  • Built-in task management and workplace news feed
  • Comprehensive labor law compliance monitoring (Core+)
  • Overtime alerts and break enforcement
  • More mature integrations ecosystem (ADP, Gusto, QuickBooks, Xero, Square)
  • Polished native mobile apps (iOS + Android)

Verdict: Deputy is stronger if you need a time clock, task management, or compliance monitoring. For AI-powered shift matching at no cost, Senzii is the clear winner. Many teams could use Senzii for scheduling and a separate time clock.

Senzii vs Sling

Sling by Toast is a budget-friendly option with a free tier (up to 30 users). It's popular in food service and retail.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching by skills, proximity, and certifications (Sling has none)
  • Coverage gap detection
  • Shift bidding functionality
  • MCP integration — manage schedules via AI assistants
  • Certification tracking

Where Sling wins:

  • Free tier for teams up to 30 users
  • Lower paid-tier pricing — $2/user Premium, $4/user Business
  • Built-in time tracking and overtime alerts (Premium+)
  • Built-in news feed for team communication
  • Native iOS and Android apps

Verdict: Sling's free tier is great for getting started with basic scheduling. Once your team needs AI matching, coverage analysis, or certification tracking, Senzii delivers more — and it's also free. Sling charges for time tracking and overtime alerts on its paid tiers; Senzii doesn't charge at all.

Senzii vs Homebase

Homebase focuses on hourly teams with hiring, scheduling, time tracking, and payroll features bundled together. Pricing is per location, not per user.

Where Senzii wins:

  • AI-powered shift matching by skills, proximity, and certifications
  • Coverage gap detection
  • Shift bidding functionality
  • MCP integration — manage schedules via AI assistants
  • Free and open-source — no per-location fees

Where Homebase wins:

  • Free Basic plan (1 location, up to 10 employees)
  • AI-powered scheduling assistant (Plus plan, $56-70/location)
  • Built-in hiring and onboarding tools
  • Integrated payroll processing ($39/mo + $6/emp add-on)
  • Built-in time clock with GPS and photo verification
  • Team communication features

Verdict: If you want an all-in-one HR platform with hiring, payroll, and time clock, Homebase covers more ground. If you specifically want AI-powered shift matching that considers skills and proximity, Senzii does that better — and it's free regardless of team size or location count.

What Makes Senzii Different

Most scheduling software gives you a digital calendar. Senzii gives you an AI scheduling engine that matches the right person to the right shift.

Free and Open-Source

Senzii is free under the Apache License 2.0. No per-seat fees, no tiered plans, no feature gates. Self-host it or use the hosted version — either way, you get the same features. Competitors charge $2.50-$9 per user per month; Senzii costs nothing.

AI Does the Matching

While competitors leave you manually assigning shifts or using basic auto-scheduling, Senzii's matching engine analyzes skills, proximity to the work site, certifications, and shift history to propose the best staff member for each shift. You review, approve, and you're done.

Talk to Your Schedule

Senzii's MCP integration lets you manage schedules by conversation. Ask ChatGPT or Claude to find coverage, swap a shift, or check tomorrow's roster — without opening an app. No other scheduling tool offers this.

Built for Multiple Industries

Senzii isn't just for retail or just for healthcare. Our AI adapts to your industry's patterns, whether you're scheduling CNAs, construction crews, warehouse shifts, or store associates.

Still Not Sure?

We won't pretend Senzii is perfect for everyone. If you need a built-in time clock, payroll processing, labor law compliance monitoring, or a massive integration marketplace, a competitor might serve you better today. But if you want AI that actually matches the best person to each shift, for free, we think Senzii is the best choice.

The best way to find out? Try it — no credit card, no trial period, no catch. It's free and open-source.
