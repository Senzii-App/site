Crew Scheduling FAQ

Common questions from contractors evaluating crew scheduling software.

What is crew scheduling software?

Crew scheduling software is a tool that helps construction and trades contractors assign workers to job sites based on trade skills, certifications, equipment expertise, and location. Unlike generic scheduling tools, crew scheduling considers which workers are qualified for specific tasks, minimizes travel time between sites, and handles last-minute callouts automatically.

How is crew scheduling different from employee scheduling?

Employee scheduling focuses on filling time slots. Crew scheduling goes further: it matches workers to job sites by trade certification, equipment expertise, and travel zone. A generic scheduler puts a body in a slot. Crew scheduling ensures the right electrician, with the right certifications, is at the right site — and has a backup plan when someone calls out.

How does crew scheduling handle last-minute callouts?

When a worker calls out, crew scheduling software automatically identifies the best replacement based on trade match, proximity to the site, availability, and shift history. Instead of the foreman making 10 phone calls, the system sends targeted notifications to qualified, available workers within travel range. First to accept gets the shift.

Can crew scheduling software track certifications?

Yes. Senzii tracks worker certifications — OSHA, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, forklift, and more — and prevents assignment to shifts that require credentials the worker does not have. Managers get alerts when certifications are expiring so they can schedule renewals before they lapse.
