Why Senzii for dental

Everything a dental practice needs to schedule efficiently

From chair assignment to recall tracking, Senzii handles the scheduling complexity so your front desk can focus on patients.

🪑

Chair Assignment Scheduling

Assign chairs to dentists and hygienists based on procedure type and estimated duration. Prevent double-booking with automatic conflict detection. Set buffer times between procedures.

🦷

Procedure-Based Time Slots

Create custom duration templates for cleanings (30 min), fillings (45 min), root canals (90 min), extractions (60 min), and bracket placements (75 min). Slots auto-adjust to procedure type.

🔄

Hygienist Rotation Management

Rotate hygienists across chairs fairly. Senzii tracks who's been in which chair, balances patient load, and ensures no hygienist is overworked or underutilized.

📅

Recall & Hygiene Intervals

Track 3-month, 6-month, or custom recall intervals. Senzii automatically flags patients due for cleaning and suggests available slots. Send batch recall reminders via SMS or email.

🚨

Emergency Slot Management

Reserve emergency time blocks throughout the day. When a patient calls with an urgent issue, instantly see available emergency slots and reshuffle non-urgent appointments.

👥

Multi-Dentist Coordination

Manage schedules for multiple dentists, specialists, and hygienists in one view. See who's available, who's double-booked, and who has gaps to fill — all on one dashboard.

🔔

Automated Patient Reminders

Reduce no-shows with automatic SMS and email reminders. Configure reminder timing (24 hours, 2 hours, or custom). Patients can confirm or reschedule with one click.

🛡️

Insurance Verification Reminders

Schedule insurance verification calls before appointments. Senzii flags patients whose insurance needs re-verification and assigns the task to front desk staff.

🍽️

Staff Lunch & Break Scheduling

Automatically schedule lunch breaks for hygienists and front desk staff. Senzii ensures chairs aren't left unattended during peak hours and breaks are distributed fairly.
