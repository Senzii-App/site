How Senzii Builds a Staff Rota

Define Your Shifts

Enter your operating hours, shift patterns, and required roles. Add credential requirements per shift (e.g., RN certification, forklift license, food handler card). Set minimum staffing levels for each role.

Set Staff Availability

Staff submit their availability, preferred shifts, and time-off requests. Senzii stores credentials, proximity zones, and shift history for each person. No manual tracking — the system keeps it current.

Generate the Rota

Senzii scores every candidate against every open shift using proximity, skills, availability, and history. The best match is assigned first, then the next, until every shift is filled or flagged as a gap. Conflicts are detected automatically.

Review & Adjust

Review the generated rota in a drag-and-drop interface. Override any assignment, swap staff between shifts, or open a gap to fill manually. Senzii re-checks compliance on every change.

Publish & Monitor

Publish the rota. Staff get notified instantly. Coverage gaps trigger alerts with suggested fill-ins. Use the Shift Swap Manager to handle any swap requests with automatic compliance validation.

Employee Rostering FAQ

What is employee rostering software?

Employee rostering software (also called rota software or staff scheduling software) automates the creation and management of staff work schedules. It assigns employees to shifts based on availability, skills, qualifications, labor laws, and business demand — replacing manual spreadsheets and paper rosters with a centralized digital system.

How is rostering different from scheduling?

Rostering focuses on which staff members are assigned to which shifts (the who), while scheduling focuses on when shifts occur (the when). In practice, modern rostering software like Senzii handles both — creating the shift structure and intelligently matching the right people to each shift based on proximity, skills, availability, and history.

How does Senzii build staff rotas?

Senzii uses intelligent matching to build rotas automatically. For each open shift, the system scores every available staff member on four signals: proximity to the work location, required skills and certifications, stated availability, and shift history. The best-matching staff member is assigned first, with conflict detection for double-bookings, overtime, and rest period violations.

Can I create recurring rota patterns?

Yes. Senzii supports rota templates for recurring weekly or biweekly patterns. Define your standard shift structure once, assign staff to recurring roles, and Senzii generates the full rota automatically — then flags exceptions like PTO requests, sick calls, or credential expirations that require adjustments.

Does rostering software handle labor law compliance?

Yes. Senzii enforces labor law compliance during roster building by checking minimum rest periods between shifts, maximum daily and weekly hours, overtime thresholds, break requirements, and maximum consecutive working days. The system flags any rota that would violate these rules before it is published.
