Yes. Senzii is a mobile-first web app that works in any browser on iPhone, Android, tablet, and desktop. Employees see their schedule, request swaps, and get push notifications without installing anything.

Yes. Employees can request shift swaps and time-off directly in the app. Managers get instant notifications and can approve or deny with one tap. Swap requests automatically check for conflicts, certifications, and overtime.
