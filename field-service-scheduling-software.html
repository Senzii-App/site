Frequently Asked Questions

What is field service scheduling software?

Field service scheduling software is a digital platform that helps trade businesses dispatch technicians to jobs efficiently. Unlike generic calendars, it factors in location, skills, certifications, parts availability, customer time windows, and travel time — automatically building schedules that minimize drive time and maximize billable hours.

How does Senzii route technicians by location?

Senzii uses proximity-based dispatch: when a new job comes in, it shows technicians within a configurable radius, sorted by travel time. You can set zones by ZIP code or draw custom service areas. For multi-day projects, the system clusters nearby jobs and flags when a technician is routed outside their assigned territory.

Can Senzii handle emergency and same-day service calls?

Yes. Senzii includes emergency dispatch mode: urgent jobs get flagged with a priority tag, and the system instantly finds the nearest available technician with the right skill and certification. If no one is free, it suggests rescheduling lower-priority appointments and notifies affected customers automatically.

How does skill and certification matching work?

Each technician profile includes skills (e.g., HVAC repair, refrigerant handling, electrical panel work) and certifications with expiration dates. Senzii only assigns jobs where the required skills and active certifications match. If a certification is expiring within 30 days, the system flags it and suggests a reassignment.

Does Senzii optimize routes to reduce drive time?

Yes. Senzii clusters jobs by geographic proximity and optimizes daily routes to minimize total travel distance. For a technician with 6 appointments, the system reorders stops to cut drive time by an average of 25–35% compared to manual scheduling — which translates to 1–2 extra billable hours per day.
