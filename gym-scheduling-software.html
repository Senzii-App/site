Frequently Asked Questions

What is gym scheduling software?

Gym scheduling software is a specialized platform for fitness businesses to manage trainer shifts, group class timetables, equipment reservations, and facility coverage. Unlike generic staff scheduling tools, gym software understands fitness-specific needs like class capacity limits, instructor certification tracking, and peak-hour staffing ratios.

Can Senzii handle group fitness class scheduling?

Yes. Senzii supports group fitness class scheduling with capacity limits, waitlist management, and instructor certification matching. Set up recurring weekly templates for yoga, spin, HIIT, CrossFit, and Pilates.

Does Senzii track personal trainer certifications?

Yes. Senzii tracks all fitness certifications — NASM, ACE, CrossFit Level 1/2, yoga alliance RYT, Pilates certification, and custom credentials. The system blocks uncertified trainers from specialized classes and sends automatic renewal reminders.

How does equipment reservation scheduling work?

Senzii integrates equipment reservation into the staff schedule. When trainers are assigned to sessions, the system reserves necessary equipment for those time slots. Members see real-time availability when booking personal training or small-group sessions.

Can members book classes through Senzii?

Yes. Senzii includes a member-facing booking portal where gym members can view the class timetable, check availability, and reserve their spot. The portal syncs with the staff schedule in real time.

How does Senzii handle peak-hour staffing?

Senzii analyzes historical foot traffic and class attendance to recommend optimal staffing ratios for each time block. The system flags under-staffed peak periods and over-staffed quiet hours.
