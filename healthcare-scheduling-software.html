Purpose-Built for Healthcare Operations

Healthcare scheduling is the most complex scheduling problem there is. Credentials, patient continuity, visit routing, compliance, on-call coverage — Senzii handles all of it.

Credential Matching

Track CNA, HHA, LPN, RN, medication aide, and specialty certifications. The system only shows caregivers whose credentials match each patient's care plan requirements — no manual checking.

Visit Route Optimization

Optimize visit sequences based on patient locations. Reduce drive time between visits. Caregivers see their route in the mobile app with estimated travel times and patient prep notes.

Patient Continuity Tracking

Prioritize assigning the same caregiver to each patient. The system warns when a patient is about to get a new caregiver and suggests the best match based on past visit history.

Secure by Design

Encrypted data in transit and at rest. Role-based access with audit logging. Senzii does not store PHI — keep scheduling data here, use HIPAA-compliant channels for protected health information.

On-Call Rotation Generator

Create fair on-call rotations with burden analysis. Track who's been on-call most, balance weekends and holidays, and ensure rest periods between on-call and regular shifts.

Compliance Alerts

Automatic alerts for missing credentials, expiring certifications, rest period violations, and overtime thresholds. Stay audit-ready without manual tracking.

EVV-Ready Time Tracking

Caregivers clock in and out at patient locations with GPS verification. Visit data is structured for Electronic Visit Verification compliance and Medicaid billing.

Caregiver Retention Tools

Track workload fairness, shift distribution, and overtime burden per caregiver. Identify burnout risk before it leads to turnover. Fair scheduling improves retention by up to 30%.

Family Communication Portal

Give families visibility into who's visiting and when — without exposing full schedule details. Automated visit confirmations and care team updates keep everyone informed.
