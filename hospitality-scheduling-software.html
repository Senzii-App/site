Purpose-Built for Hospitality Operations

Hotels and resorts have the most complex scheduling challenge — multiple departments, 24/7 operations, seasonal swings, and event-driven demand. Senzii handles all of it.

Multi-Department Scheduling

Schedule housekeeping, front desk, F&B, maintenance, spa, and security from one dashboard. Each department has its own shift patterns, skill requirements, and coverage rules.

24/7 Coverage Management

Handle overnight shifts, weekend coverage, and holiday staffing. Track rest periods, flag consecutive night shifts, and ensure minimum coverage per department at all times.

Seasonal Demand Scaling

Adjust staffing levels based on occupancy forecasts. Scale up for peak season, down for off-season. Create special templates for conferences, weddings, and holiday events.

Multi-Property Support

Transfer staff between properties during shortages. Compare labor costs across locations, standardize scheduling practices, and manage your entire portfolio from one view.

Housekeeping Room Assignment

Assign rooms to housekeepers with checkout/checkin times and priority flags. Balance room counts per housekeeper and account for VIP assignments and deep cleans.

Event-Driven Staffing

Create event-specific schedules for conferences, weddings, and banquets. Add banquet servers, setup crews, and security with role-based assignments and event timelines.

Occupancy-Based Forecasting

Link staffing to occupancy forecasts. Senzii suggests optimal staffing levels based on expected occupancy, checkin/checkout patterns, and historical demand data.

Break & Rest Compliance

Automatic break scheduling based on shift length and state labor laws. Track consecutive shifts, ensure minimum rest periods, and flag compliance risks before they escalate.

Mobile App for Staff

Staff see their schedule, swap shifts, request time off, and pick up open shifts from their phone. Push notifications for schedule changes reduce no-shows and improve communication.
