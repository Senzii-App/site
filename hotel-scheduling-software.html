The problem

Hotel scheduling is harder than it looks

Hotels run 24/7 across multiple departments with different staffing needs, skill requirements, and labor rules. Generic scheduling tools can't handle the complexity. Spreadsheets can't keep up with last-minute changes.

  • 24/7 coverage gaps. Front desk and security need overnight coverage. Housekeeping peaks at checkout time. F&B spikes during breakfast and dinner. One missed gap means guest complaints.
  • Department silos. Front desk, housekeeping, F&B, and maintenance each have their own scheduling needs. Managing them separately means blind spots. Managing them together in one spreadsheet is chaos.
  • Seasonal volatility. Conference season, holidays, and summer peaks double your staffing needs overnight. Your core team can't cover it alone — but on-call pools are hard to manage without the right tools.
  • Labor cost leaks. Overtime creeps in when coverage gaps get filled with whoever's available. Without per-department cost tracking, you find out at month-end that housekeeping blew its budget.
  • Last-minute changes. Call-outs, no-shows, and sudden group bookings demand instant re-scheduling. Phone trees take hours. Staff don't see updated schedules until they show up.

The solution

How Senzii solves hotel scheduling

Senzii treats every department as its own scheduling world — then connects them so you see the full property at a glance.

Department-based scheduling

Create separate shift patterns for front desk, housekeeping, F&B, maintenance, and security. Each department has its own skill requirements, coverage rules, and staffing levels — but you see everything in one unified property schedule.

24/7 coverage planning

Build overnight shifts that span midnight. Set minimum rest periods between consecutive shifts. Automatically detect coverage gaps in any time block — front desk at 3 AM, housekeeping during checkout rush, security on weekends.

Skills & certifications

Track food safety certifications, first aid training, language skills, and any custom credential. Senzii prevents assigning staff to shifts they're not qualified for — no expired food handler's card on the breakfast buffet.

Labor cost tracking

See labor costs per department, per shift, and per day in real time. Set budget alerts for each department. Overtime warnings fire before the shift is assigned — not after the timesheet comes in.

On-call & seasonal pools

Maintain a pool of on-call workers for peak seasons, conferences, and events. When a gap opens, Senzii suggests available pool members based on skills, proximity, and past performance. Fill gaps in minutes, not hours.

Mobile app for staff

Housekeepers and maintenance staff get room assignments on their phone. Front desk sees live coverage status. Shift swaps and time-off requests happen in the app — no phone trees, no paper requests, no confusion.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Senzii handle 24/7 hotel scheduling with overnight shifts?

Yes. Senzii is built for round-the-clock operations. You can create overnight shifts spanning midnight, set minimum rest periods between shifts, and automatically detect coverage gaps in any time block. The system ensures front desk, security, and housekeeping are always covered.

Does Senzii support department-based scheduling for hotels?

Yes. Senzii lets you organize staff by department — front desk, housekeeping, food and beverage, maintenance, security, and more. Each department has its own shift patterns, skill requirements, and coverage rules. You can view schedules by department or see the full property schedule side by side.

How does Senzii handle seasonal staffing for peak hotel periods?

Senzii supports seasonal staffing through on-call pools and temporary staff management. You can create a pool of on-call workers for peak seasons, conferences, or events, and automatically fill gaps from the pool based on availability, skills, and proximity to the property.

Can Senzii track housekeeping room assignments and progress?

Yes. Senzii assigns housekeeping staff to rooms based on room type, checkout times, and VIP status. Staff receive their room assignments on the mobile app, mark rooms as cleaned, and flag maintenance issues in real time. Front desk sees live status updates.

How does Senzii handle event and group booking staffing?

Senzii lets you create event-specific shift blocks tied to group bookings or conferences. When a large group books, you can pre-schedule the additional F&B, housekeeping, and front desk staff needed, with automatic overlap planning to ensure seamless service during peak event hours.
