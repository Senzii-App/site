What makes scheduling "intelligent"?

Skills & Certification Matching

Senzii checks every staff member's certifications before assigning a shift. An unlicensed electrician never gets sent to a job that requires one. A CNA without the right specialty never gets assigned to a patient who needs specialized care.

Proximity-Based Routing

The closest qualified staff member gets the shift. Not the one the manager remembers. Not the one who called last. Senzii minimizes travel time and maximizes the chance the person actually shows up.

Availability Awareness

Senzii only proposes staff who are actually available — no PTO conflicts, no double-booking, no "I thought she was off." Real-time shift acceptance and rejection keeps the schedule live.

Shift History & Continuity

The same caregiver who visited last week gets priority for this week's shift. The same crew that knows the equipment gets routed back. Continuity isn't just nice — it reduces no-shows and improves quality.

How it works

Senzii proposes. You approve. The shift is filled.

1

Enter the shift

Date, time, location, required skills. That's it. Senzii pulls in everything it knows about your staff.

2

Senzii scores every available staff member

Each candidate is ranked on the four signals: skills match, proximity, availability, and shift history. The best match rises to the top.

3

You approve (or adjust)

Senzii shows you the proposed match with the reasoning. Approve it, swap someone in, or let Senzii auto-assign. Your call.

4

The shift is filled — and tracked

Confirmation goes out. If someone calls out, Senzii automatically routes the next-best match. No phone trees. No panic.

What is intelligent staff scheduling?

Intelligent scheduling uses algorithms to automatically match the best available staff member to each open shift — considering skills, certifications, proximity, availability, and shift history. Instead of a manager manually reviewing who's available and qualified, the system proposes the optimal match for approval.
