Frequently Asked Questions

What is labor scheduling software?

Labor scheduling software is a tool that helps managers create, publish, and manage employee shift schedules. It replaces spreadsheets and manual scheduling with automated coverage detection, overtime prevention, compliance checking, and real-time team notifications.

How does labor scheduling software reduce labor costs?

Senzii reduces labor costs by preventing unplanned overtime (alerting before a shift triggers OT), identifying overstaffing through coverage grids, and reducing no-shows through automated reminders. Most teams see 15-30% reduction in overtime costs within the first month.

Can labor scheduling software handle multiple locations?

Yes. Senzii supports multiple locations with separate schedules, employee pools, and coverage requirements. You can schedule across sites or keep them isolated depending on your needs.
