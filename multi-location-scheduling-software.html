Scheduling Across Multiple Locations Shouldn't Mean Multiple Spreadsheets

Managing staff schedules across multiple locations is one of the hardest operational challenges in shift-based industries. Each site has different staffing needs, credential requirements, and coverage gaps. When schedulers use separate spreadsheets or standalone tools per location, they miss opportunities to share staff across sites, prevent coverage gaps, and reduce overtime costs.

Senzii brings every location into a single, intelligent scheduling system. Managers see all schedules at once, identify coverage gaps across sites, and assign the right staff to the right location based on proximity, skills, and availability — automatically.

📍 Location-Aware Matching

Senzii scores every available staff member against every open shift at every location. Proximity, required credentials for that site, and travel time are all factored in — so you never assign someone to a site they can't reach.

🔄 Cross-Location Staff Sharing

When one location has a gap and another has surplus, Senzii surfaces it. Move staff between sites without losing credential compliance or creating travel-time conflicts.

📊 Centralized Dashboard

View all locations in one dashboard. Filter by site, date, or staff member. See coverage gaps, overtime risks, and credential compliance across your entire operation at a glance.

🏷️ Per-Location Credentials

Different sites have different requirements. A healthcare location may require specific certifications. A construction site may require equipment proficiency. Senzii tracks per-location credential requirements and prevents mismatches.

⏱️ Travel Time Protection

Senzii calculates travel time between locations and flags impossible back-to-back assignments. No more scheduling a staff member at Site A at 8am and Site B at 9am when they're 45 minutes apart.

🔔 Cross-Location Alerts

When a location has a coverage gap, Senzii alerts you and suggests staff from nearby locations who are qualified and available. Fill gaps in minutes, not hours.

Multi-Location Scheduling FAQ

What is multi-location scheduling software?

Multi-location scheduling software lets managers coordinate staff shifts across multiple sites or branches from a single dashboard. It handles cross-location coverage, travel time between sites, per-location credential requirements, and centralized reporting so franchise and multi-site operations do not need separate scheduling systems for each location.

How does Senzii handle multi-location staff scheduling?

Senzii matches staff to shifts based on proximity to each location, required certifications for that site, availability, and shift history. Managers can assign staff across locations, detect coverage gaps at any site, and view all schedules in a centralized dashboard with location-based filtering.

Can staff work at multiple locations?

Yes. Senzii tracks each staff member's eligible locations and travel zones. Staff can be assigned to shifts at different locations on different days, and the system calculates travel time between locations to prevent impossible back-to-back assignments.
