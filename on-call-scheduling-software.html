The On-Call Problem

At 5:47 AM, a caregiver calls in sick. Their first visit is at 7:00. You need a replacement with the right certification, close to the client, available this morning. In most organizations, this means calling down a list—hoping someone picks up, hoping they have the right credentials, hoping they are close enough to make the visit on time.

The Phone-Tree Tax

Managers spend an average of 25–40 minutes resolving a single callout. During that time, the shift is uncovered, the client may cancel, and the manager is not doing anything else. For agencies with 5+ daily callouts, that is 2–3 hours of manager time lost every day—just to coverage gaps.

Senzii eliminates the phone tree. When a shift opens, the system automatically identifies the best replacement and sends the offer. If the first worker declines, it cascades to the next best match—no manual intervention needed.

Key Features

Automatic Callout Routing

When a worker calls out, Senzii instantly identifies the best replacement based on proximity, skills, availability, and overtime status. No phone tree needed.

On-Call Rotation Management

Set up rotation schedules for who is first responder. Define rotation frequency, blackout periods, and standby compensation rules. Workers see their on-call weeks in advance.

Shift Cascade

If the first-choice replacement declines, Senzii automatically sends the offer to the next best match. The shift stays covered without a manager in the loop.

Certification Verification

Only workers with valid, non-expired certifications for the shift's requirements are included in the candidate pool. Compliance is automatic, not manual.

Real-Time Overtime Alerts

Before an on-call assignment is confirmed, Senzii checks the worker's current hours. If the assignment would trigger overtime, the system flags it and suggests alternatives.

Mobile Shift Acceptance

Workers receive push notifications for on-call offers. They accept or decline with one tap. No phone tag, no voicemail, no waiting.
