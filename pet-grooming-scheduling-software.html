Why Senzii for pet grooming

Everything a pet salon needs to schedule efficiently

From breed-based groomer assignment to mobile route optimization, Senzii handles the scheduling complexity so your front desk can focus on pets and customers.

🐕

Groomer Assignment by Pet Size & Breed

Match groomers to appointments based on pet size (small, medium, large, XL) and breed-specific requirements. Set each groomer's experience level, breed specialties, and size preferences to ensure every pet gets the right groomer.

⏱️

Appointment Duration Estimation

Automatically estimate grooming duration based on breed, pet size, and service type. A small breed bath takes 30 minutes while an XL breed full groom needs 2.5 hours. Customize duration templates for every breed and service combination.

✂️

Breed-Specific Service Scheduling

Create service templates tailored to breed grooming standards. Poodle cuts, terrier hand-stripping, breed-specific trims, and specialized coat treatments each get their own duration, pricing, and skill requirements.

👥

Multi-Groomer Coordination

Manage schedules for multiple groomers in one view. See who's available, who's double-booked, and who has gaps to fill. Balance workload fairly and ensure no groomer is overbooked or sitting idle.

🚐

Mobile Grooming Route Management

Optimize daily routes for mobile groomers. Senzii sequences appointments by location to minimize drive time, accounts for travel between stops, and adjusts the schedule when appointments run long or customers reschedule.

🏠

Boarding & Daycare Scheduling

Schedule boarding stays and daycare sessions alongside grooming appointments. Track kennel capacity, manage check-in and check-out times, and coordinate feeding and exercise schedules for boarded pets.

💉

Vaccination Tracking

Record and track vaccination records for every pet. Senzii flags pets with expired vaccinations, blocks boarding and daycare bookings until records are updated, and sends automatic reminders to customers when vaccines are due.

📱

Customer Self-Booking Portal

Let pet owners book their own appointments online. The portal shows available time slots that match the groomer's breed and size qualifications, lets customers choose services and preferred groomers, and handles reschedules and cancellations.

🔄

Recurring Appointment Management

Set up recurring grooming schedules for regular customers — every 4 weeks, 6 weeks, or custom intervals. Senzii auto-generates future appointments, sends reminders before each visit, and fills any cancellations automatically.
