Why Senzii for pharmacies

Everything a pharmacy needs to schedule staff efficiently

From pharmacist rotations to 24/7 coverage planning, Senzii handles the scheduling complexity so your pharmacy manager can focus on patient care.

💊

Pharmacist Rotation Management

Schedule pharmacist rotations across day, evening, and overnight shifts. Senzii balances workload fairly, tracks hours to prevent burnout, and ensures every shift has a licensed pharmacist on duty.

🕐

Technician Shift Scheduling

Create technician shifts that align with pharmacist coverage. Assign techs to dispensing, inventory, and counter roles. Track certifications and ensure only qualified techs handle specific tasks.

🌙

24/7 Coverage Planning

Ensure continuous coverage for 24-hour pharmacies. Senzii maps every hour of the week to a scheduled pharmacist and technician, flagging any gaps before they happen. Overlapping shifts for smooth handoffs.

📞

On-Call Rotation

Manage on-call pharmacist schedules for after-hours emergencies. Senzii rotates on-call duty fairly, tracks response times, and sends automatic alerts to the on-call pharmacist when they're needed.

🍽️

Lunch Break Coverage

Automatically schedule lunch break coverage so the pharmacy counter is never left unattended. Senzii staggers break times, assigns coverage techs, and ensures a pharmacist is always available during breaks.

💉

Immunization Clinic Scheduling

Schedule dedicated immunization clinic blocks without disrupting regular pharmacy operations. Assign certified pharmacists to vaccine clinics, track certifications, and manage off-site vaccination events.

📋

Regulatory Compliance Tracking

Track state-specific pharmacist-to-technician ratios, break requirements, and overtime limits. Senzii flags schedules that violate regulations before they're published, keeping your pharmacy audit-ready.

🏊

Float Pharmacist Pool Management

Create a float pharmacist pool to cover staffing gaps across multiple locations. Senzii tracks float availability, travel times, and home store assignments to suggest the best float for each gap.

🏪

Cross-Coverage Between Locations

Coordinate staff who work across multiple pharmacy locations. Senzii tracks which pharmacist is at which store on which days, prevents travel-time conflicts, and balances cross-coverage assignments fairly.

🏪

Retail Chain Pharmacies

Schedule pharmacists and technicians across multiple store locations. Manage float pools, cross-coverage assignments, and per-store staffing ratios. Senzii handles transfer requests and travel time between stores.

🏥

Hospital Pharmacies

Coordinate inpatient pharmacy staff with clinical rounds, IV admixture schedules, and cart-fill times. Manage 24/7 coverage with on-call pharmacists for emergency medication orders and code responses.

🏪

Independent Community Pharmacies

Schedule a small team of pharmacists and techs with fair rotations. Track lunch break coverage, manage part-time staff, and ensure your pharmacy is always staffed during business hours without overpaying.

24-Hour Pharmacies

Plan continuous 24/7 coverage with day, evening, and overnight shifts. Senzii ensures smooth shift handoffs, tracks overnight on-call availability, and prevents gaps in pharmacist coverage during low-traffic hours.
