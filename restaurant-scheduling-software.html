Built for Restaurant Operations

Restaurants don't schedule like other businesses. You have stations, split shifts, doubles, seasonal surges, and a mix of full-time and part-time staff. Senzii handles all of it.

Station-Based Matching

Tag every staff member by station — server, bartender, line cook, prep, dishwasher, host, busser, barista. Shifts are auto-matched to station requirements so you never schedule a cook on the bar.

Split Shifts & Doubles

Support for split shifts (lunch + dinner with a gap), doubles (lunch through dinner), and standard single shifts. Total hours tracked per day and week to flag overtime before it happens.

FOH/BOH Coordination

Balance front-of-house and back-of-house coverage ratios. Senzii warns when you're top-heavy on servers but short on cooks — before the dinner rush.

Seasonal Surge Templates

Pre-built templates for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Thanksgiving, New Year's Eve, and summer patio season. Add temporary staff, increase shift counts, and auto-assign based on demand.

Shift Swaps & Open Shifts

Staff request swaps through the mobile app. Managers approve with one tap. Open shifts broadcast to qualified staff — first eligible to accept gets it. No more group chat chaos.

POS Integration

Sync with Toast, Square for Restaurants, and Lightspeed Restaurant. Use actual sales data to forecast labor needs and schedule based on real revenue patterns, not guesses.

Labor Cost Tracking

See real-time labor cost as a percentage of sales. Senzii flags when you're over the 30% danger zone and suggests where to trim without sacrificing service quality.

Break & Meal Compliance

Automatic break and meal period scheduling based on state labor laws. Senzii flags missing breaks, late meals, and compliance risks before they become labor violations.

Mobile App for Staff

Staff see their schedule, request time off, swap shifts, and pick up open shifts — all from their phone. Push notifications for schedule changes and shift reminders reduce no-shows.
