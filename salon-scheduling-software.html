Frequently Asked Questions

What is salon scheduling software?

Salon scheduling software is a digital platform that helps salon owners, managers, and stylists manage appointments, booth rentals, walk-in clients, and staff schedules. Modern tools like Senzii go beyond simple booking calendars by tracking client history, service preferences, commission splits, and product sales — reducing no-shows, maximizing chair utilization, and improving the client experience.

Can Senzii handle booth rental salons where stylists are independent?

Yes. Senzii supports booth rental and chair rental models where stylists operate as independent contractors. Each stylist gets their own schedule, client list, and commission/tip tracking. The salon owner sees an aggregate dashboard of all booth renters, can enforce shared policies (like hours of operation or cleanliness standards), and track overall occupancy and revenue — without micromanaging individual stylists.

How does walk-in queue management work?

Senzii's walk-in queue lets front desk staff (or stylists themselves) add walk-in clients to a digital waitlist with estimated wait times based on current service durations. Clients receive SMS updates as their turn approaches, and stylists see the queue on their dashboard. When a no-show or cancellation opens a slot, the system can automatically offer it to the next walk-in — keeping chairs full and revenue flowing.

Can clients book online without calling the salon?

Yes. Senzii generates a branded booking page for your salon that clients can access via your website, Instagram bio link, or Google Business Profile. Clients see real-time availability by stylist or service type, book their preferred slot, receive automatic confirmation and reminder texts, and can reschedule or cancel without calling. This reduces front-desk phone volume by 40–60% while capturing bookings 24/7.

How does commission and tip tracking work?

Senzii tracks every service, product sale, commission split, and tip per stylist. You can set tiered commission rates (e.g., 40% for junior stylists, 50% for senior stylists, 60% for master stylists), handle product retail commissions separately, and record cash or card tips. Daily and weekly payout reports show exactly what each stylist is owed — eliminating manual calculation errors and payout disputes.

Does Senzii support recurring bookings and membership clients?

Yes. Clients can set up recurring appointments (e.g., every 4 weeks for color touch-ups, every 2 weeks for barber cuts). Senzii automatically reserves their preferred time slot, sends reminder notifications, and flags conflicts if the stylist becomes unavailable. For membership or package clients, the system tracks remaining credits, applies them automatically at checkout, and alerts both client and salon when a package is about to expire.
