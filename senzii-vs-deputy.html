Why Teams Switch from Deputy to Senzii

1. AI Scheduling That Actually Works

Deputy's schedule builder is polished, but every shift assignment is manual. Senzii's AI auto-matching considers skills, availability, certifications, labor laws, and even travel time across locations — then suggests the optimal employee for each shift. One click replaces 30 minutes of manual work.

2. No Feature Tiers

Deputy splits features across Standard and Premium. Need analytics, certification tracking, or advanced reporting? That's Premium at $8/user. Senzii includes everything at $5/seat. No tiers, no upsells, no paying extra for features you need.

3. Comprehensive Compliance

Deputy offers basic overtime alerts. Senzii includes real-time compliance monitoring: overtime prevention, mandatory break enforcement, certification expiration tracking, and state-specific labor law alerts. For healthcare and construction teams, this prevents costly violations.

4. Coverage Gap Detection

Deputy shows your schedule but doesn't proactively identify problems. Senzii detects uncovered shifts, understaffed periods, and overstaffed times — with actionable recommendations to fix them before they impact operations.

5. Photo Verification Time Clock

Deputy has an excellent GPS time clock (one of their best features). Senzii matches the GPS capability and adds photo verification — so you know who actually clocked in, not just where they were.

6. Predictable Pricing at Scale

At 50 employees, Deputy Premium costs $400/month. Senzii costs $250/month for the same team with all features included. The gap widens as you grow — Senzii's flat pricing scales linearly without surprise tier upgrades.

When Deputy Might Be the Better Choice

Fair comparison — Deputy has real strengths:

Built-in Task Management

Deputy includes task assignment and checklists within shifts. Senzii integrates with external tools for task management. If your team needs tasks tied directly to shifts, Deputy's built-in approach is cleaner.

Workplace News Feed

Deputy has a built-in communication channel (News feed) for announcements and team updates. Senzii uses Slack or Teams integrations. Some teams prefer an all-in-one platform.

More Payroll Integrations

Deputy integrates with Xero, Gusto, Square, and other payroll providers that Senzii doesn't yet support. If your payroll system isn't QuickBooks or ADP, Deputy has broader coverage.

Polished Mobile Experience

Deputy's mobile app is one of the best in the industry — refined over years of development. Senzii's app is modern and capable, but Deputy's polish shows in edge cases and offline scenarios.

How to Switch from Deputy to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Deputy

In Deputy, go to Locations → Export to download your employee data, areas, and shift patterns. Deputy provides CSV exports for all core data.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Deputy's "Areas" to Senzii locations, and shift patterns to templates. The wizard flags any mismatches for review.

Step 3: Configure Compliance Rules

Set up your state's labor law parameters — overtime thresholds, break requirements, rest periods. Senzii's compliance engine runs automatically on every schedule.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Click "Auto-Generate" and let Senzii's AI build the optimal schedule. Compare it to your Deputy schedule — most teams find the AI version covers more shifts with fewer conflicts.

Step 5: Cancel Deputy

Once your team is active on Senzii, cancel your Deputy subscription. No cancellation fees. Your historical data stays available in Deputy for 90 days after cancellation.
