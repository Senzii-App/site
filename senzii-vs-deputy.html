Why Teams Switch from Deputy to Senzii

1. AI-Powered Shift Matching

Deputy's auto-scheduling (Core+) uses rules and templates. Senzii's AI matching engine — find_candidates and the assignment engine — considers skills, availability, certifications, and coverage needs to suggest the optimal employee for each shift. One click replaces 30 minutes of manual work. And it's free and open-source.

2. Free and Open-Source

Senzii is free under the Apache License 2.0. No per-seat fees, no tiers, no add-ons, no trial limitations. Deputy charges $4.50–$9/user/month, plus add-ons for payroll ($8/user + $49/mo base), HR ($2/user), messaging ($1.95/user), and analytics ($1.50/user). For a 50-person team on Deputy Pro, that's $450/month before add-ons. Senzii costs $0.

3. MCP Integration — Manage Schedules via ChatGPT or Claude

Senzii supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so you can manage schedules through AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude. Ask your AI assistant to find coverage, check gaps, or assign shifts — all through natural language. Deputy does not offer MCP integration.

4. Coverage Gap Detection

Deputy shows your schedule but doesn't proactively identify problems. Senzii detects uncovered shifts and understaffed periods — with actionable recommendations to fix them before they impact operations.

5. Shift Bidding

Senzii includes shift bidding, letting employees bid on open shifts based on their availability and qualifications. Deputy does not currently offer shift bidding — it relies on shift swap and find replacement features instead.

6. Full Data Ownership

Senzii is open-source — you can self-host and retain complete control of your workforce data. No vendor lock-in, no data export limits, no subscription to cancel. Deputy is proprietary SaaS; your data lives on their servers and access depends on your subscription.

When Deputy Might Be the Better Choice

Fair comparison — Deputy has real strengths that Senzii doesn't match yet:

GPS Time Clock with Biometrics

Deputy's time clock is one of the best in the industry — GPS verification, facial recognition biometrics, and geofencing (Core+). Senzii does not have a time clock. If built-in time clocking is essential, Deputy is the clear winner.

Labor Law Compliance Monitoring

Deputy includes labor law compliance on all plans, with advanced compliance on Core and Pro. This includes overtime alerts, break enforcement, and compliance reporting. Senzii does not currently offer compliance monitoring. For regulated industries, Deputy is the safer choice.

Demand Forecasting & Labor Optimization

Deputy Core+ includes demand forecasting, traffic-based staffing, and labor optimization with wage and labor budgets. Senzii does not offer demand forecasting. If you need data-driven staffing predictions, Deputy delivers.

Built-in Task Management

Deputy includes task assignment and checklists within shifts. Senzii does not have built-in task management. If your team needs tasks tied directly to shifts, Deputy's built-in approach is cleaner.

Native iOS & Android Apps

Deputy offers native iOS and Android apps — refined over years of development, with offline support and push notifications. Senzii is a mobile-first web app, not native. For teams that need a native mobile experience, Deputy is stronger.

Payroll Integrations

Deputy integrates with QuickBooks, ADP, Xero, Gusto, and Square, plus offers Deputy Payroll as an add-on. Senzii does not currently have payroll integrations. If seamless payroll processing is a must, Deputy has broader coverage.

Workplace News Feed & Messaging

Deputy has a built-in communication channel (News feed) for announcements and team updates, plus Messaging+ as an add-on. Senzii does not have built-in team messaging. Some teams prefer an all-in-one platform.

Advanced Analytics & Reporting

Deputy includes basic reporting on Lite and advanced analytics via the Analytics+ add-on. Senzii does not have an advanced analytics dashboard. For teams that need deep labor cost reporting and performance insights, Deputy is the better option.

How to Switch from Deputy to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Deputy

In Deputy, go to Locations → Export to download your employee data, areas, and shift patterns. Deputy provides CSV exports for all core data.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Deputy's "Areas" to Senzii locations, and shift patterns to templates. The wizard flags any mismatches for review.

Step 3: Set Up Certifications & Availability

Configure employee certifications, availability, and skills in Senzii. These power the AI matching engine — the more accurate your data, the better the shift suggestions.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Click "Auto-Generate" and let Senzii's AI build the optimal schedule. Compare it to your Deputy schedule — most teams find the AI version covers more shifts with fewer conflicts.

Step 5: Cancel Deputy

Once your team is active on Senzii, cancel your Deputy subscription. No cancellation fees. Your historical data stays available in Deputy for 90 days after cancellation. Since Senzii is open-source, your data stays yours.
