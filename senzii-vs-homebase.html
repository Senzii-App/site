Why Teams Switch from Homebase to Senzii

1. AI Scheduling Replaces Manual Work

Homebase requires manual schedule building on every plan — free, Essentials, and Plus. Senzii's AI auto-matching considers skills, availability, certifications, and labor laws — then assigns the optimal employee to each shift automatically. One click replaces 30 minutes of dragging and dropping. No Homebase plan offers this.

2. Transparent Pricing Without Base Fees

Homebase charges a monthly base fee ($20 for Essentials, $40 for Plus) on top of per-user pricing. A 20-person team on Plus pays $140/month — $40 more than Senzii for fewer features. Senzii is $5/seat flat with zero base fees and every feature included. No tiers, no upselling.

3. Compliance That Prevents Lawsuits

Homebase offers basic overtime alerts on Essentials and Plus. Senzii includes real-time compliance monitoring: overtime prevention, mandatory break enforcement, certification tracking with expiration alerts, and state-specific labor law alerts. For teams in regulated industries (healthcare, construction, food service), this prevents costly violations that Homebase can't catch.

4. Multi-Location Without Paying for Plus

Homebase limits free plan users to a single location. Multi-location support requires the Plus plan at $40/month + $5/user — making it the most expensive option. Senzii includes full multi-location scheduling at $5/seat with no base fee. Cross-location scheduling, location-based roles, and coverage analysis across sites are all included.

5. Photo Time Clock at No Extra Cost

Homebase offers GPS time clock only on Plus. Senzii includes GPS + photo verification at $5/seat — no buddy-punching, accurate time tracking, and seamless payroll export. Photo clock-in isn't available on any Homebase plan.

6. API Access for Custom Workflows

Homebase doesn't offer API access on any plan. Senzii includes full API access at $5/seat, enabling custom integrations, automated reporting, and workflow automation. Connect Senzii to your existing stack without waiting for Homebase to add a native integration.

When Homebase Might Be the Better Choice

Homebase has genuine advantages in certain scenarios:

Free Tier for Small Single-Location Teams

If you have fewer than 20 employees at one location and only need basic scheduling + time clock, Homebase's free tier is genuinely free. It includes scheduling, a basic time clock, and team communication. For tiny teams with simple needs, $0/month is hard to beat. Senzii starts at $5/seat.

Built-in Hiring and Onboarding

Homebase Plus includes job posting, applicant tracking, and new hire onboarding — features Senzii doesn't offer natively. If hiring is a primary need alongside scheduling, Homebase offers an all-in-one HR platform that may reduce your tool stack.

Built-in Payroll Processing

Homebase Plus includes integrated payroll processing (at additional cost per run). If you want scheduling and payroll from the same vendor, Homebase offers that convenience. Senzii integrates with QuickBooks and ADP for payroll but doesn't process payroll itself.

POS Integrations for Restaurants

Homebase has deeper POS integrations (Square, Toast, Clover) built in, which can streamline sales-labor forecasting for restaurants. Senzii integrates via Zapier but doesn't have native POS integrations yet.

How to Switch from Homebase to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Homebase

In Homebase, go to Settings → Export Data. Download your employee list, locations, and shift schedules as CSV files. Homebase allows data export on all plans.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Homebase locations to Senzii locations, and shift schedules to schedule templates. The wizard handles field mapping automatically and flags any mismatches.

Step 3: Set Up Compliance Rules

Configure your state's labor law parameters — overtime thresholds, break requirements, rest periods. This is something Homebase doesn't offer, so it's a new capability for your team. Senzii's state labor law lookup tool can help you identify requirements.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Click "Auto-Generate Schedule" and let Senzii's AI build the optimal schedule. Compare it to your Homebase schedule — most teams find the AI version covers more shifts with fewer conflicts and lower overtime costs.

Step 5: Cancel Homebase (If Paid)

If you were on Homebase Essentials or Plus, cancel your subscription. If you were on the free plan, simply stop using it. Your Senzii schedule is live with all features active — no tiers, no upselling.
