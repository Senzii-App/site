Quick Verdict

Senzii wins on AI-powered shift matching by skills, proximity, and certifications (free vs Homebase's AI scheduling assistant on Plus only), coverage gap detection, shift bidding, MCP integration, and price (free vs $24-120/location/month). Homebase wins on breadth: built-in time clock with GPS and photo, hiring and onboarding, integrated payroll, POS integrations, team messaging, and native mobile apps. For AI scheduling at no cost, Senzii is the clear choice. For an all-in-one HR platform, Homebase covers more ground.

Why Teams Switch from Homebase to Senzii

1. AI Matches People to Shifts

Homebase's AI Scheduling Assistant (Plus plan only) helps build schedules. Senzii's AI goes further — it matches specific people to specific shifts based on skills, proximity to the work site, certifications, and shift history. The best candidate for each shift, proposed automatically. Free, no Plus plan required.

2. Free and Open-Source

Homebase charges $24-120 per location per month, plus $39/month + $6/employee for payroll. Senzii is free and open-source under the Apache License 2.0. No per-location fees, no per-employee fees, no tiers. Self-host it or use the hosted version — same features either way.

3. Talk to Your Schedule

Senzii's MCP integration lets you manage schedules by conversation. Ask ChatGPT or Claude to find coverage, swap a shift, or check tomorrow's roster — without opening an app. No other scheduling tool, including Homebase, offers this.

4. Coverage Gap Detection

Homebase shows you your schedule but doesn't proactively identify problems. Senzii detects uncovered shifts, understaffed periods, and overstaffed times — with actionable recommendations to fix them before they impact operations.

5. Shift Bidding

Senzii includes shift bidding functionality that Homebase doesn't offer. Let staff bid on open shifts based on their qualifications, and Senzii's AI ranks candidates by match score — skills, proximity, and history.

6. No Location Limits

Homebase charges per location — a 5-location team pays 5 subscriptions. Senzii includes multi-location support for free, with cross-location scheduling and coverage analysis across all sites.

When Homebase Might Be the Better Choice

Homebase has genuine advantages — here's where it wins:

Built-in Time Clock

Homebase includes a time clock with GPS and photo verification. Senzii doesn't have a time clock yet. If accurate time tracking is critical to your operation, Homebase has this covered.

Hiring and Onboarding

Homebase All-in-One includes job posting, applicant tracking, and new hire onboarding — features Senzii doesn't offer. If hiring is a primary need alongside scheduling, Homebase is an all-in-one HR platform.

Integrated Payroll

Homebase processes payroll in-house ($39/month + $6/employee). Senzii doesn't process payroll. If you want scheduling and payroll from the same vendor, Homebase offers that convenience.

POS Integrations

Homebase integrates with Square, Toast, and Clover POS systems for sales-labor forecasting. Senzii doesn't have native POS integrations yet.

Team Messaging

Homebase includes built-in team communication. Senzii focuses on scheduling and integrates with external tools for messaging.

Native Mobile Apps

Homebase has native iOS and Android apps. Senzii uses mobile-first web apps — they work in any browser, but some teams prefer a native app experience.

How to Switch from Homebase to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Homebase

In Homebase, go to Settings → Export Data. Download your employee list, locations, and shift schedules as CSV files. Homebase allows data export on all plans.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Homebase locations to Senzii locations, and shift schedules to schedule templates. The wizard handles field mapping automatically and flags any mismatches.

Step 3: Set Up Staff Profiles

Verify employee profiles, add certifications, and set availability. Senzii's matching engine uses this data to propose the best person for each shift.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Let Senzii's AI build a schedule draft. It matches staff to shifts by skills, proximity, and certifications. Review, adjust, and publish. Your team gets instant notifications.

Step 5: Cancel Homebase (If Paid)

If you were on a Homebase paid plan, cancel your subscription. If you were on the free plan, simply stop using it. Your Senzii schedule is live — and it's free.
