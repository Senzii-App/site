Why Teams Switch from Sling to Senzii

1. AI Scheduling Replaces Manual Work

Sling's free and Pro plans both require manual schedule building. Senzii's AI auto-matching considers skills, availability, certifications, and labor laws — then assigns the optimal employee to each shift automatically. One click replaces 30 minutes of dragging and dropping.

2. Compliance That Prevents Lawsuits

Sling offers basic overtime alerts on Pro. Senzii includes real-time compliance monitoring: overtime prevention, break enforcement, certification tracking, and state labor law alerts. For teams in regulated industries (healthcare, construction, food service), this prevents costly violations.

3. GPS Time Clock Included

Sling doesn't offer a time clock at all — not on free, not on Pro. Senzii includes a GPS-enabled time clock with photo verification at $5/seat. No buddy-punching, accurate time tracking, and seamless payroll export.

4. Analytics Without Paywalls

Sling reserves most analytics for Pro, and even then the reporting is limited. Senzii includes full labor cost reporting, coverage analysis, schedule optimization scores, and trend analysis for every customer.

5. API Access for Custom Workflows

Sling doesn't offer API access on any plan. Senzii includes full API access at $5/seat, enabling custom integrations, automated reporting, and workflow automation.

6. Real Integrations

Sling's integrations are limited to POS systems on Pro. Senzii integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams, QuickBooks, ADP, Google Calendar, and Zapier (connecting to 5,000+ apps) — all included at $5/seat.

When Sling Might Be the Better Choice

Sling has genuine advantages in certain scenarios:

Free Tier for Very Small Teams

If you have fewer than 10 employees and only need basic scheduling (no time clock, no compliance, no analytics), Sling's free tier is genuinely free. You can't beat $0/month. Senzii starts at $5/seat, so for tiny teams with simple needs, Sling Free is the right choice.

Built-in Team Messaging

Sling includes chat and news features built in. If your team doesn't use Slack or Teams and wants scheduling + messaging in one app, Sling offers that convenience.

Restaurant-Focused Features

Sling was built with restaurants in mind and has features tailored to food service. While Senzii serves restaurants well, Sling's POS integrations and recipe for restaurant scheduling workflows are well-tested.

How to Switch from Sling to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Sling

In Sling, go to Settings → Export. Download your employee list, locations, and shift templates as CSV files.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Sling's locations to Senzii locations, and shift templates to schedule templates. The wizard handles field mapping automatically.

Step 3: Set Up Compliance Rules

Configure your state's labor law parameters — overtime thresholds, break requirements, rest periods. This is something Sling doesn't offer, so it's a new capability for your team.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Click "Auto-Generate Schedule" and let Senzii's AI build the optimal schedule. Compare it to your Sling schedule — most teams find the AI version covers more shifts with fewer conflicts and lower overtime costs.

Step 5: Cancel Sling (If Paid)

If you were on Sling Pro, cancel your subscription. If you were on Sling Free, simply stop using it. Your Senzii schedule is live with all features active.
