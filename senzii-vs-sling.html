Quick Verdict

Both are free — but in different ways. Senzii is free and open-source (Apache 2.0) with no user limits, AI-powered shift matching, coverage gap detection, shift bidding, and MCP integration for managing schedules via ChatGPT or Claude. Sling offers a free tier for up to 30 users with basic scheduling, built-in team messaging, and native iOS/Android apps, plus paid plans that add time tracking and kiosk clock-in. Choose Senzii for AI scheduling and open-source flexibility. Choose Sling if you need time tracking, built-in messaging, or native mobile apps.

Why Teams Switch from Sling to Senzii

1. AI Shift Matching — Free

Sling doesn't offer AI scheduling on any plan — free or paid. Senzii's AI-powered matching engine assigns the best employee to each shift based on skills, availability, and certifications. One click replaces manual scheduling work, and it's completely free and open-source.

2. MCP Integration for AI Assistants

Senzii includes MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration, so you can manage schedules through ChatGPT or Claude. Ask your AI assistant to find coverage, check gaps, or generate a weekly schedule — all without leaving your chat. Sling has no equivalent capability.

3. Coverage Gap Detection

Sling shows your schedule but doesn't proactively identify understaffed periods. Senzii detects coverage gaps automatically and flags them before they impact operations. Know exactly where you're short — and get AI-recommended candidates to fill the gaps.

4. Shift Bidding

Senzii includes shift bidding — let employees bid on open shifts and the system matches the best candidate automatically. Sling only offers basic shift swaps. For teams with frequent open shifts, bidding streamlines the process significantly.

5. Certification Tracking

Senzii tracks employee certifications and alerts you before they expire. This is critical for healthcare, food service, and construction teams. Sling does not offer certification tracking on any plan.

6. Open-Source & Self-Hostable

Senzii is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. You can self-host it, modify it, and contribute back. No vendor lock-in, no surprise price increases, no per-seat fees — ever. Sling is proprietary and you're dependent on their pricing decisions.

When Sling Might Be the Better Choice

Fair comparison — Sling has real strengths that Senzii doesn't match yet:

Time Tracking & Time Clock

Sling offers mobile time tracking on Premium ($2/user/month) and a kiosk time clock on Business ($4/user/month). Senzii does not have a time clock, time tracking, or overtime alerts. If time tracking is essential, Sling's paid plans are the clear choice.

Built-in Team Messaging

Sling includes chat, group messaging, and a newsfeed on all plans — even the free tier. Senzii doesn't have built-in messaging. If your team wants scheduling and communication in one app without using Slack or Teams, Sling is more convenient.

Native iOS & Android Apps

Sling has polished native mobile apps for iOS and Android. Senzii is a mobile-first web app — it works great on phones, but it's not a native app. If a native app experience is important to your team, Sling delivers that.

Drag-and-Drop Schedule Builder

Sling's drag-and-drop schedule builder is intuitive and well-designed. Senzii focuses on AI-generated schedules and doesn't yet offer a drag-and-drop interface. If you prefer manual visual scheduling, Sling's builder is smoother.

Payroll Integrations

Sling's Business plan includes payroll reports and integrations. Senzii doesn't currently integrate with payroll providers. If you need end-to-end scheduling-to-payroll workflows, Sling Business covers that.

How to Switch from Sling to Senzii

Step 1: Export from Sling

In Sling, go to Settings → Export. Download your employee list, locations, and shift templates as CSV files.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

Use Senzii's CSV import wizard. Map Sling's locations to Senzii locations, and shift templates to schedule templates. The wizard handles field mapping automatically.

Step 3: Set Up Certifications & Availability

Add certification data and availability preferences for each employee. Senzii's AI matching uses these to assign the right person to every shift.

Step 4: Generate Your First AI Schedule

Click "Auto-Generate Schedule" and let Senzii's AI build the optimal schedule. Compare it to your Sling schedule — most teams find the AI version covers more shifts with fewer conflicts.

Step 5: Stop Using Sling

If you were on Sling Free, simply stop using it. If you were on a paid plan, cancel your subscription. Your Senzii schedule is live with all features active — at no cost.
