Why Teams Switch from When I Work to Senzii

1. AI-Powered Shift Matching

When I Work's Auto Scheduling fills shifts based on availability and roles. Senzii goes further with a find_candidates + assignment engine that scores each candidate by skills, certifications, and availability — matching the right person to the right shift, not just any available person.

2. Free & Open-Source

When I Work charges $2.50–$8 per user per month, every month, forever. Senzii is free and open-source under the Apache License 2.0. No per-seat fees, no tiers, no base fees. A 20-person team saves $600–$1,920 per year. You can self-host it and own your data.

3. MCP Integration with ChatGPT & Claude

Senzii supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), so you can manage schedules directly from ChatGPT or Claude. Ask your AI assistant to find coverage, check gaps, or assign shifts — without opening a separate app. When I Work has no equivalent integration.

4. Coverage Gap Detection

When I Work shows you your schedule but doesn't proactively warn you about gaps. Senzii automatically detects uncovered shifts and understaffed periods so you can fix them before they cost you.

5. Shift Bidding

Senzii includes shift bidding, letting employees bid on open shifts so managers can assign based on preferences and qualifications. When I Work's OpenShifts feature allows claiming shifts but doesn't support a bidding workflow.

6. Certification Tracking Built In

Senzii tracks employee certifications and skills as part of its matching engine. When I Work does not offer certification tracking. For teams in healthcare, food service, or regulated industries, this matters — Senzii won't assign an employee to a shift their certifications don't cover.

When When I Work Might Be the Better Choice

We're transparent. Here's where When I Work has an edge today:

GPS Time Clock & Geofencing

When I Work includes a time clock with GPS and geofencing on all plans — employees can only clock in from approved locations. Senzii does not have a time clock feature. If time tracking is essential, When I Work is the clear choice.

Native iOS & Android Apps

When I Work offers native mobile apps for iOS and Android with push notifications, offline access, and a polished mobile experience. Senzii is a mobile-first web app — it works on any phone with a browser, but it's not a native app.

Built-in Team Messaging

When I Work includes team messaging on all plans — no need for a separate Slack or Teams subscription. Senzii does not have built-in messaging. If you want scheduling and chat in one app, When I Work delivers.

Payroll & POS Integrations

When I Work integrates with payroll providers (Rippling partnership) and POS systems. Senzii does not have payroll integrations. If you need to push time data to payroll automatically, When I Work has this built in.

Drag-and-Drop Scheduling UI

When I Work's drag-and-drop schedule builder is polished and intuitive. Senzii does not yet have a drag-and-drop interface — schedules are generated and managed through its matching engine and templates.

Large Integration Ecosystem

When I Work integrates with Slack, Teams, Zapier, and dozens of other tools. Senzii's integration story is currently limited to MCP. If you need a broad integration marketplace, When I Work has more options today.

How to Switch from When I Work to Senzii

Step 1: Export from When I Work

Go to When I Work → Settings → Export Data. Export your employee list, shift templates, and time-off policies as CSV files.

Step 2: Import to Senzii

In Senzii, go to Settings → Import. Upload your CSV files. Senzii maps fields automatically and flags any conflicts for review.

Step 3: Review & Adjust

Verify employee profiles, set up your locations, and confirm certifications and availability. Most teams complete this in 30 minutes.

Step 4: Build Your First AI-Matched Schedule

Use Senzii's find_candidates and assignment engine to match employees to shifts based on skills, certifications, and availability. Review, adjust, and publish. Your team gets instant notifications.

Step 5: Cancel When I Work

Once your team is on Senzii, cancel your When I Work subscription. No cancellation fees, no data loss — your Senzii schedule is live and free forever.
