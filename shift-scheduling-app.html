Key Features

Mobile Push Notifications

Workers get notified the moment a shift they qualify for opens up. No need to check a board or a group chat. One tap to accept or decline.

Smart Shift Matching

The app doesn't just show open shifts — it ranks them by fit. A CNA sees CNA shifts first. A forklift operator sees warehouse shifts first. Relevance, not noise.

Self-Service Shift Swaps

Workers propose swaps from their phone. The app validates certifications, checks for conflicts, and alerts the manager for one-tap approval.

Overtime Guardrails

Before any shift is confirmed, the app checks the worker's current weekly hours. If the shift would trigger overtime, it flags it and suggests alternatives.

Certification Blocking

Workers without the required certification simply cannot claim the shift. This prevents safety violations, compliance issues, and manager anxiety.

Real-Time Schedule Sync

Changes propagate instantly. If a manager updates a shift, every affected worker sees it immediately. No version conflicts, no "I didn't get the memo."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Senzii truly mobile-friendly?

Yes. Senzii is built mobile-first. Workers can view their schedule, accept shifts, request swaps, and check their hours from any smartphone. Managers can create shifts, approve swaps, and monitor coverage from their phone.

Can workers use Senzii without downloading an app?

Yes. Senzii is a progressive web app (PWA) that works in any modern browser. Workers can bookmark it to their home screen for an app-like experience without visiting an app store.

Does Senzii work offline?

Workers can view their cached schedule offline. Changes sync automatically when connectivity returns. This is critical for field workers in areas with spotty coverage.

How fast can Senzii fill an open shift?

For shifts with standard requirements, Senzii typically identifies and notifies the best match within 5 seconds. The worker can accept with one tap. Total time from "shift opens" to "shift covered" is usually under 60 seconds.
