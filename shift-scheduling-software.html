Shift Scheduling Software FAQ

What is shift scheduling software?

Shift scheduling software is a tool that helps managers create, manage, and communicate employee shift assignments. Unlike manual spreadsheets, shift scheduling software like Senzii automates the process of matching employees to shifts based on skills, availability, and labor law requirements — while preventing conflicts like overtime, double-bookings, and coverage gaps before schedules are published.

How does Senzii's shift scheduling work?

Senzii uses AI-powered matching to assign the right employee to each shift. You define your staffing needs (roles, number of employees, time windows), and Senzii automatically matches employees based on their skills, certifications, availability, and location. It flags compliance issues like overtime thresholds or insufficient rest periods before you publish — so your schedules are always compliant and fully staffed.

Can employees swap shifts using shift scheduling software?

Yes. Senzii includes a built-in shift swap system where employees can request swaps from their mobile app. Senzii automatically validates each swap against 10 compliance rules: shift ownership, double-bookings, daily/weekly hour limits, overtime, rest periods, consecutive days, role qualifications, and fairness. Managers get an approve/deny recommendation instantly.

How much does shift scheduling software cost?

Senzii costs $5 per seat per month with no contracts, no setup fees, and no per-location charges. All features are included: shift building, compliance monitoring, shift swaps, mobile notifications, coverage gap detection, and reporting. You can start with a free trial and cancel anytime.

Does shift scheduling software work for 24/7 operations?

Yes. Senzii supports 24/7 shift patterns including overnight shifts, rotating schedules, on-call rotations, and split shifts. You can set up recurring shift templates for continuous coverage, define minimum rest periods between shifts, and track cumulative hours across days and weeks to prevent fatigue and overtime violations.

Can I manage shifts for multiple locations?

Yes. Senzii supports unlimited locations with location-specific shift rules, staffing requirements, and time zones. You can view all shifts from one dashboard, filter by location, and move staff between sites when coverage gaps arise. Cross-location compliance rules ensure employees aren't double-booked across sites.
