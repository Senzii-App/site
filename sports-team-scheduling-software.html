Frequently Asked Questions

What is sports team scheduling software?

Sports team scheduling software is a digital platform that helps coaches, athletic directors, and league administrators create, distribute, and manage game schedules, practice plans, and travel itineraries. Modern tools like Senzii go beyond simple calendars by automatically detecting venue conflicts, assigning referees, managing field/court rotations, and sending notifications to players, parents, and staff — reducing manual work and eliminating scheduling errors that cost games.

Can Senzii handle multiple teams and age groups at once?

Yes. Senzii supports unlimited teams, age groups, and divisions under a single organization account. A high school athletic director can manage varsity, junior varsity, and freshman schedules across all sports simultaneously. A youth league can handle U8, U10, U12, U14, and U16 divisions with separate field assignments, referee pools, and playoff brackets — all from one dashboard.

How does venue conflict detection work?

Senzii maps every venue (fields, courts, rinks, pools) with availability windows, blackout dates, and shared-use rules. When you schedule a game or practice, the system instantly checks for double-bookings, maintenance conflicts, and overlapping reservations. If a conflict exists, it flags the event and suggests alternative venues or time slots — preventing the all-too-common scenario of two teams showing up for the same field.

Can parents and players see schedules without an account?

Yes. Every team gets a public shareable schedule link that parents and players can bookmark on any device — no login required. For privacy, you can optionally password-protect the link or require email verification. Coaches can also push schedule updates via SMS, email, or team chat apps so nobody misses a last-minute field change.

Does Senzii support tournament and playoff bracket generation?

Yes. Senzii includes tournament scheduling with automatic bracket generation for single-elimination, double-elimination, round-robin, and pool-play formats. The system seeds teams based on season standings, assigns courts/fields for each round, and updates brackets in real-time as results come in. Referees and scorekeepers are auto-assigned based on availability and certification level.

How does travel scheduling work for away games?

Senzii's travel itinerary builder links each away game to departure times, bus assignments, hotel reservations, and meal stops. It calculates travel time between venues and automatically buffers for traffic, check-in, and warm-up. Athletic directors can see all team travel on a unified map view, preventing bus or hotel double-bookings across multiple teams.
