Frequently Asked Questions

What is staff scheduling software?

Staff scheduling software is a digital platform that helps managers create, distribute, and manage employee work schedules. Unlike basic spreadsheets, modern staff scheduling software like Senzii auto-matches employees to open shifts based on their skills, certifications, and availability — cutting manual work by up to 80% and reducing scheduling errors.

How does Senzii auto-match staff to shifts?

Senzii analyzes each employee's profile — skills, certifications, availability, location, and preferences — and suggests the best candidate for every open shift. Managers can approve or override suggestions. This eliminates the manual process of cross-referencing spreadsheets, cutting schedule-building time from hours to minutes.

Can Senzii handle multiple locations?

Yes. Senzii supports unlimited locations with independent shift patterns, coverage requirements, and billing rates. You can assign staff to specific sites, allow cross-site coverage when qualified, and view real-time coverage across all locations from a single dashboard.

Does Senzii track staff certifications?

Yes. Every employee profile stores certifications, licenses, and training records with expiry dates. Senzii automatically flags expiring credentials and prevents scheduling employees on shifts that require expired qualifications — protecting your organization from compliance violations and liability.

How does Senzii prevent overtime?

Senzii tracks daily and weekly hours for every employee in real time. Before publishing a schedule, it flags shifts that would push a worker into overtime and suggests alternative assignments. Managers can set custom overtime thresholds and receive alerts when employees approach their limits.

Is there a mobile app for staff?

Yes. Senzii has a mobile app for iOS and Android that lets employees view schedules, request time off, swap shifts, and check in with GPS verification. Managers can approve requests, receive no-show alerts, and update schedules from anywhere.
