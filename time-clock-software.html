Time Clock FAQ

Common questions about digital time clocks, answered.

What is time clock software?

Time clock software is a digital system that records when employees clock in and out, tracks breaks, calculates hours worked, and often integrates with payroll systems. Modern solutions include GPS verification, photo capture, and automatic overtime alerts.

Do I need special hardware for a digital time clock?

No. Modern time clock software works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer. Employees can clock in via a mobile app or web browser. Some businesses use a shared tablet at a central location, but no dedicated hardware is required.

How does GPS time clock verification work?

GPS time clock verification uses the employee's phone location to confirm they are at the correct job site when clocking in. Managers set geofences around work locations. If an employee tries to clock in outside the geofence, the system flags it for review.

Can time clock software prevent buddy punching?

Yes. Photo verification, biometric integration, and GPS geofencing all reduce or eliminate buddy punching. Senzii offers optional photo capture on every clock-in, making it easy to verify the right person is at the right location.

Does time clock software integrate with payroll?

Yes. Quality time clock software exports timesheets in formats compatible with QuickBooks, ADP, Gusto, and other payroll systems. Senzii calculates regular hours, overtime, and breaks automatically and exports clean CSV or direct API data.
