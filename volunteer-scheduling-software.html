Features Built for Volunteer Management

AI Shift Matching

Senzii automatically proposes the best volunteer for each shift based on skills, past performance, availability, and preferences. No more manual matching — the system learns your volunteers' patterns and gets smarter over time.

Instant Coverage for Cancellations

When a volunteer cancels, Senzii automatically identifies qualified available replacements and sends them notifications. No more calling down a list at 8 PM on a Friday. The system fills gaps in minutes, not hours.

Volunteer Self-Service App

Volunteers manage their own availability, pick up open shifts, request swaps, and receive reminders — all from the iOS or Android app. Coordinators stop being the bottleneck for every schedule change.

Multi-Location & Event Scheduling

Manage volunteers across multiple sites, events, or programs. Assign volunteers to specific locations, track coverage per site, and get organization-wide visibility. Perfect for food banks, shelters, festivals, and multi-site nonprofits.

Skill & Certification Tracking

Track volunteer skills, certifications, and training completion. Senzii ensures only qualified volunteers are matched to shifts that require specific skills — critical for healthcare volunteering, food handling, and working with vulnerable populations.

Compliance & Background Checks

Track background check expiration dates, required training, and compliance documents. Senzii alerts you when a volunteer's certification is about to expire and prevents scheduling volunteers with lapsed compliance requirements.

Communication & Reminders

Automated shift reminders via SMS and email reduce no-shows by up to 60%. Broadcast announcements to all volunteers or targeted groups. Two-way messaging keeps everyone connected without sharing personal contact info.

Volunteer Hours Tracking

Automatically log volunteer hours by shift. Export reports for grant applications, annual reports, and impact measurement. Track individual volunteer engagement over time to recognize your most dedicated contributors.
