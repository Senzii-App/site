Features

Built for the realities of warehouse operations

🔄

24/7 Shift Rotation Scheduling

Support for 2-2-3, 2-3-2, 4-on-4-off, and custom rotation patterns. Auto-generate the full calendar, track shift equity across teams, and ensure minimum staffing per shift.

🚪

Dock Door Scheduling

Assign inbound and outbound appointments to specific dock doors. Schedule loading/unloading crews, prevent double-booking, and track appointment duration by load type.

🗺️

Zone-Based Labor Allocation

Define zones (receiving, putaway, picking, packing, shipping, returns) and assign workers per shift. Track zone coverage, balance workload, and dynamically reallocate when demand shifts.

📋

Certification Tracking

Track forklift, scissor lift, pallet jack, and other equipment certifications with expiry dates. Prevent uncertified operators from being assigned to restricted tasks. Get expiry alerts in advance.

⏰

Overtime Control

Senzii flags potential overtime before it happens — when a worker is approaching 40 hours, when a shift extension would trigger OT, and when rotation patterns create chronic OT exposure.

👷

Temp Worker Management

Mark workers as temp, set their agency, track hours separately, and generate agency billing reports. Auto-fill coverage gaps with available temp workers based on skills and availability.

交接

Cross-Shift Handover

Manage shift transitions with digital handover notes. Outgoing shift logs incomplete tasks, incoming shift sees priorities. No more "I didn't know dock 4 was backed up" moments.

🍽️

Break & Lunch Scheduling

Stagger breaks and lunches to maintain zone coverage. Senzii automatically schedules break windows based on shift start times and coverage requirements — no more zone gaps during lunch.

⚖️

Labor Law Compliance

Senzii enforces meal break requirements, rest periods, maximum consecutive shifts, and mandatory days off based on state and federal labor laws. Compliance violations are flagged before they happen.

Built for every type of warehouse operation

E-Commerce Fulfillment

Distribution Centers

Inbound receiving, cross-docking, and outbound shipping with dock scheduling. Manage 24/7 operations with shift rotations and zone-based labor allocation.
