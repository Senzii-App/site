Workforce Management Software FAQ

What is workforce management software?

Workforce management software is a platform that helps organizations schedule employees, track labor costs, monitor compliance, and optimize staffing levels. Unlike simple scheduling tools, workforce management software like Senzii integrates demand forecasting, skills matching, labor law compliance, and real-time analytics into one system — so managers can make data-driven decisions about who works when and where.

How is workforce management different from employee scheduling?

Employee scheduling focuses on creating shift assignments. Workforce management goes further by integrating scheduling with labor cost tracking, compliance monitoring, demand forecasting, and performance analytics. Senzii combines both: you get intelligent scheduling plus the management tools to track labor budgets, prevent overtime, and ensure compliance across all locations.

Can workforce management software reduce labor costs?

Yes. Senzii reduces labor costs by preventing unnecessary overtime (average 25-30% reduction), eliminating overstaffing through demand-based scheduling, and reducing no-shows through automated shift notifications. The real-time labor cost dashboard shows cumulative spend per employee, department, and location so managers can adjust before budgets are exceeded.

Yes. Senzii supports unlimited locations with location-specific rules, time zones, staffing requirements, and budget tracking. You can manage all locations from one dashboard, filter staff by worksite, set location-based certifications, and compare labor costs across sites.
